## The Business Question

Disney has released 573 movies spanning 1937 to 2016 across multiple genres. The question was: **which genres have been truly profitable when inflation is considered, and what patterns in release timing drive maximum revenue?**

## What I Did

Analyzed Disney's complete movie catalog using Power BI, examining genre profitability, release trends, and revenue patterns across eight decades.

Key analysis areas:

- Genre profitability with and without inflation adjustment
- Release timing patterns by month and day of week
- Year-over-year revenue trends from 1937 to 2016
- Top earning movies by total and inflation-adjusted gross

## Key Findings

- **Adventure** dominates total gross revenue at $24.6bn — 43.75% of all revenue
- **Musical genre** is surprisingly the most profitable when inflation is applied — only 16 movies generating $9.7bn gross, with Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs alone earning $5.2bn inflation-adjusted
- Disney had maximum releases — 113 movies — between 1990 and 2000
- **March and November** are peak release months — Spring Break and Holiday season drive maximum revenue
- **Fridays** are the preferred release day — but 9 Tuesday releases made significant average gross revenue
- Post-2000 Disney shifted strategy toward Adventure and Action, moving away from Musicals which once drove their highest per-movie returns

- **Power BI** — interactive dashboard with genre, release and revenue analysis
- **DAX** — inflation adjustment calculations and YoY% measures
- **Data Analysis** — trend identification across 80 years of movie data
